# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
React (Vite) Frontend, Node.js + Prisma Backend. PostgreSQL Database.

## Users
Customers seeking premium, handcrafted jewelry for everyday wear or special occasions.
Admin staff operating the store, managing inventory, orders, and promotions.

## Product Purpose
To provide a seamless, premium online shopping experience for handcrafted jewelry, offering everything from standard catalog browsing to a custom "Box Builder" experience. Success means high conversion rates, clear visual hierarchy, and an effortless checkout flow.

## Positioning
Malkincraft stands out by offering genuinely handcrafted, premium jewelry with a distinctly warm, minimal aesthetic. The custom Box Builder mechanism allows for personalized curation that neighboring products lack.

## Operating Context
Customers browse predominantly on mobile devices and desktop browsers. Admins operate through a secure, data-rich admin dashboard to manage complex catalogs, attributes, and user analytics.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Full e-commerce capabilities: cart, wishlists, reviews, Razorpay checkout, dynamic filtering, promotions, OTP-based auth, and automated emails.
Constraint: Must maintain the "Premium + Handcrafted + Minimal + Warm" aesthetic across all surfaces.

## Brand Commitments
Voice: Elegant, warm, minimal, and premium.

## Evidence on Hand
Various high-quality product images (via Unsplash stubs), categorized into Bracelets, Necklaces, etc.
Real functional flows for checkout, authentication, and product discovery.

## Product Principles
1. **Elevate the Craft:** The interface should recede to let the high-quality product imagery and handcrafted nature of the jewelry shine.
2. **Warm Minimalism:** Use whitespace and typography to create a premium feel without being stark or cold.
3. **Frictionless Commerce:** From discovery to checkout, the path to purchase must be effortless and trustworthy.
